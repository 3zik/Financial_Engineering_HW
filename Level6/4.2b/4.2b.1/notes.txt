Why?

A static member of a class template is not one variable shared by every Array<T> ever made. It is one static variable per instantiation.

So basically, Array<int> and Array<double> are two different classes to the compiler.

Because of this intArray1 and intArray2 are both Array<int>, and so they both jump to 15 because they share the Array<int>::s_defaultSize.
doubleArray does not share this with them, so it stays at 10 and never sees the change.
