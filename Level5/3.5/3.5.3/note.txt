Will the proper destructors be called (before making ~Shape() virtual)?

No. delete shapes[i] on a Shape* only calls ~Shape(), so the dtor is resolved from the pointer's type. 
This leads to incorrect dtors being called.

Are the proper dtors called after declaring Shape() as virtual?

Yes. After using the virtual keyword, we construct the vtable and every dtor in the chain fires correctly.
So, it called derived class dtor, then base class dtor for every object.
